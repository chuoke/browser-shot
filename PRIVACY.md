# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**BrowserShot Webpage Screenshot / BrowserShot 网页截图**

Last updated / 最后更新:2026-09-27

## English

BrowserShot is a webpage screenshot browser extension. **It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data.**

- All processing happens locally in your browser. Captured screenshots, capture history, settings and presets are stored only on your device (via `chrome.storage` and IndexedDB) or saved to your Downloads folder when you choose to download.
- The extension makes **no network requests** and contains **no remote code, analytics, ads, or trackers**.
- It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no telemetry of any kind.
- Browser permissions are used solely to perform captures you initiate (reading the active tab's pixels, injecting a selection overlay, saving files, showing notifications). Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you have questions, please open an issue on the project repository or contact the developer.

## 中文

BrowserShot 是一款网页截图浏览器扩展。**它不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享任何用户数据。**

- 所有处理均在浏览器本地完成。截图、截图历史、设置与预设仅保存在你的设备上(`chrome.storage` 与 IndexedDB),或在你主动下载时保存到下载目录。
- 扩展**不发起任何网络请求**,不包含远程代码、统计、广告或追踪器。
- 无需账号,无需登录,无任何形式的遥测。
- 浏览器权限仅用于执行你主动发起的截图操作(读取当前标签页画面、注入选择框、保存文件、显示通知),不会产生任何外发数据。

如有疑问,请在项目仓库提交 issue 或联系开发者。
